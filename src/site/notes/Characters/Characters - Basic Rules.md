title: Characters - Basic Rules
---

In Modern Canterbury Tales, characters make up a party, similar to other tabletop games. Before each campaign, all participants must provide the following to the DM:

- A brief 1-2 paragraph bio about their character. 
- 3-5 pictures of objects found in real life. These items can be around your house or anywhere outside. These can pretty much be as small as a thumbtack or as big as a building. As long as the picture is clear and is a picture of something real it will be accepted.

After receiving all sets of characters from each campaign participant, the DM will use the Campaign Generator Tool to generate each character's stats and starting items.
