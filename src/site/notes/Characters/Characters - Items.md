title: Characters - Items
---

Similar to other tabletop RPGs, in Modern Canterbury Tales, items are used to buff character stats, heal allies, damage enemies, and much more. Items are either one-time use or attachable assets. If the item indicates a buff to stats like
	`Border-Steel Blade | Weapon | +15 Attack
assume this is an attachable asset with unlimited uses. Many items do not buff character stats. If these items do not indicate only a certain number of uses before it vanishes, assume it has unlimited amount of uses. There might be some special cases where an attachable item (like armor, weapons, etc.) might have a certain number of hits before it vanishes. Please consult the instructions provided by the Campaign Creator tool for generated items in these cases.

There is no limit to the number of items a character can hold in Modern Canterbury Tales. Additionally, all attachable assets add to character stats. There's no concept of "equipping" items; they are always equipped and all characters gain the buffs from them. For example, if a character has the following items in their inventory:

	Border-Steel Blade | Weapon | +15 Attack
	Reinforced Chainmail | Armor | +15 Defense
	Marcher’s Boots | Footwear | +10 Speed
	Treatise on Border Law | Book | +5 Diplomacy
	Refined Whetstone | Item | +5 Attack

And the character's base stats are

	Health 30
	Attack 20
	Defense 10
	Speed 5
	Diplomacy 10

Then the characters full stats are

	Health 30
	Attack 40 (20 + 15 + 5)
	Defense 25 (10 + 15)
	Speed 15 (5 + 10)
	Diplomacy 15 (10 + 5)
