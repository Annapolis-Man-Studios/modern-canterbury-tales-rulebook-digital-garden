title: Characters - Stats
---
Each character comes with 5 different types of stats:

- Health - The amount of health points your character has before dying. Unless there are outstanding circumstances, if tjeor HP goes to 0, a character is eliminated from the campaign
- Attack - The amount of attack points a character inflicts in battle. Attack points are subtracted from the receiver's HP
- Defense - The amount of defense points a character has in battle. To calculate total damage of an attack, take the (inflicter's attack - battle target's defense) and subtract that from the battle target's health. If attack - defense is a negative number, no damage is inflicted to the battle target
- Speed - Determines which battle participant performs their action first in a battle phase
- Diplomacy - The ability to convince enemies to retreat in battle. If a character's diplomacy stat is *greater than or equal to* an enemy's diplomacy stat, the enemy will fold and leave the battle immediately

A character possesses each of these base stats, which players can add on to as the campaign progresses.
