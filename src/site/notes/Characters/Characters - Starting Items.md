title: Characters - Starting Items
---

Every character has 3-5 starting items. These starting items are generated by Campaign Creator tool based on the character description and the images provided with the character description.

Here's an example of a generated starting item:

	|**Item Name:** Zevo Instant Purifier|
	|Description: A clear spray bottle filled with a potent, natural-smelling formula designed to eliminate small, bothersome creatures. The label proudly states, "Kills on Contact!" This mid-game utility item provides tactical options against a variety of foes.|

	|Effects:|
	|* Offensive Spray: Deals 25 damage to a single enemy. If the target is an "Insectoid" or "Parasitic" enemy type, this item deals an additional 15 damage and reduces their Defense by 5 for 2 turns.|
	|* Disorienting Mist: Can be used to spray an enemy's face, inflicting no damage but reducing their Speed by 10 for 1 turn.|
	|Use Limit: This item has 7 charges.|
