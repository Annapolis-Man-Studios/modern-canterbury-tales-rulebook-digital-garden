title: Battles - Crafting
---
Canonically, scientists in the present day have figured out how to conjure powerful magic by drawing it into existence in the world of 500 AD Wales/England. This is a powerful tool party participants can utilize. In Modern Canterbury Tales, players can utilize this special technique that creates one-use items that can impact battles significantly. To cast a craft in battle, the following needs to occur:
- Player declares to use a craft on N number of targets during the Decision Phase
- When a player executes their craft in the battle phase, the player must use a physical or digital canvas to draw on. The player has 1 minute to draw their creation
- After the creation is drawn, the drawing is sent as an image to an AI prompt and output is created
- Please follow the directions provided in the response

*NOTE: Each player can use up to 2 crafts per dungeon/terrain unless otherwise specified. Each player can use up to 1 craft per sidequest requiring a battle.*
