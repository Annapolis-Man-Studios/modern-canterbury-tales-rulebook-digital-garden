title: Battles - Running
---

Players can also choose to run from battles. Players can decide whether to run individually or as a group.

To calculate whether the individual player can run, the DM compares the player's speed stat to the combined speed stat of all enemies. If the individual's speed stat is *greater than or equal to the summation of the speed stats of all enemies*, the individual is able to run from battle. In this case, the player will not be in battle anymore but the current battle will continue until either all players have run, all players have fallen, or all enemies have fallen. 

In the event where all players wish to escape the battle, the DM compares the summation of the speed stats of all participating players to the summation of the speed stats of the enemies. If the summation of player speed stats is *greater than or equal to summation of enemy speed stats*, the party successfully escapes the battle.

In the case where all players fall and other players have escaped the battle, the DM will evaluate the win cases. If the win cases have not been achieved, the game will continue.

After all battles escaped in dungeons, all players must choose an adjacent area to move as a group. The party may not ask for clues about these areas either. Additionally, any battles escaped in terrains will end the current turn immediately and reset the battle in the designated area. Any battles from sidequests escaped in this manner will also end the turn immediately.
