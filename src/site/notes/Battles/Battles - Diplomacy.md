title: Battles - Diplomacy
---

Diplomacy is the primary way to convince enemies to stop fighting and run away from battle. Generally, if the player using diplomacy on an enemy has a diplomacy value *greater than or equal to the enemy's diplomacy value*, the enemy forfeits and leaves the battle immediately. If the player's diplomacy value is less than the enemy's, the player's speed decreases by 10 for the next turn. Additionally, there are many bosses where diplomacy will not work. For example, any boss that has a diplomacy stat that looks like the following:

	Diplomacy: 1 (Cannot be negotiated with)

The "Cannot be negotiated with" overrides the numerical value. However, this can be overridden with discretion by the DM at any time.
