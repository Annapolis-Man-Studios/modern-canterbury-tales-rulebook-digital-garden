title: Battles - Attacking + Defending
---

Attacking is the primary action for dealing damage to a battle target. If a player is attacking, they can attack any battle participant (including their allies) unless otherwise specified. The modified attack stat (base stat + attachable items) of the character is used to inflict damage to the receiving participant.

Defending doubles your defense during the Battle Phase. After defending once, the player cannot defend until one turn has passed.
