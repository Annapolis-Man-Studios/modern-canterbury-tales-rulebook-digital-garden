title: Battles - Basic Rules
---

In Modern Canterbury Tales, players will engage in battles with enemy monsters controlled by the DM similar to other tabletop games. Battles are turn-based battles where the turns are comprised of a few different phases in this order:
- Decision Phase
- Preparation Phase
- Battle Phase
- Repeat

The battle ends in two different cases:
- If all enemies fall - battle ends and surviving players receive benefits.
- If all players fall - battle ends and everyone loses the game.

Here's a brief description of each of these phases:
- In the Decision Phase, each player and enemy declares the action they will be taking each turn. The enemy actions are not revealed until the Battle Phase begins.
- During the Preparation Phase, the DM then takes the speed stats of each of the players and the enemy and orders them from largest to smallest. Unless an item is used to increase or reduce speed values, this is the order actions will be executed during the Battle Phase. If an enemy and a player have the same speed, allow the enemy to use their action first unless there is an item saying otherwise.
- During the Battle Phase, all of the actions of each battle participant are executed in order of each participant's speed.

Participants in each battle can take any of the following actions per turn:
- Attacking
- Item Usage
- Diplomacy
- Flank Movement (declaring front flank, back flank, or middle flank)
- Crafting
- Running
