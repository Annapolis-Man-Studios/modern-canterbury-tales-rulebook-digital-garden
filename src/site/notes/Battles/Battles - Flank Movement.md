title: Battles - Flank Movement
---

Modern Canterbury Tales also has a mechanic where players and enemies can choose positions in battle. We call these "flanks". These flanks determine how much additional damage is dealt to and from other battle participants. Here's an overview of each flank:
- Middle Flank - the default position of all battle participants. Nothing abnormal occurs unless an item/status indicates otherwise
- Front Flank - a position that's in front of the party. In this position, a battle participant inflicts double the damage to battle targets, but also receives double the damage from battle targets. For example, for a player/enemy that is in front flank and has 20 attack, the inflicting attack will be 40 damage - the battle target's defense stat. If a battle target is inflicting damage on a player in front flank, the player will be dealt the (battle target's attack x 2) - the player's defense to their Health stat
- Back Flank - a position that's behind the rest of the party. In this position, a battle participant only inflicts half the damage to battle targets, but also only receives half of the damage from battle targets. For example, for a player/enemy that is in back flank and has 20 attack, the inflicting attack will be 10 damage - the battle target's defense stat. If a battle target is inflicting damage on a player in back flank, the player will be dealt the battle (target's attack / 2) - the player's defense to their Health stat

Battle participants can only move between adjacent flanks. The flank layout is the following:

	--------------------------------------------------------------------
	Front Flank
	--------------------------------------------------------------------
	Middle Flank (players/enemies start here every battle)
	--------------------------------------------------------------------
	Back Flank
