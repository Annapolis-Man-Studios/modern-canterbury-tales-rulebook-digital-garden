---

In order to play this version of the game, there should be a Dungeon Master (DM) and at least 2-10 players. The DM should be using the "Modern Canterbury Tales: Campaign Creator" tool to customize the campaign to their liking. 

Before the game starts, each participant will have to provide the following to the DM:

- A brief 1-2 paragraph bio about their character.
    
- 3-5 pictures of objects found in real life. These items can be around your house or anywhere outside. These can pretty much be as small as a thumbtack or as big as a building. As long as the picture is clear and is a picture of something real it will be accepted.
    
- The DM will auto generate stats, items, and a “special move” for each participant. The DM can generate these using the tool mentioned above.
    

*Full disclosure: all player/enemy stats, town layouts and dungeon layouts will be auto-generated via scripts utilizing premade Google Gemini prompts. The Gemini model currently used is [gemini-3.1-flash-lite](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.1-flash-lite). This simplifies the enemy/town/dungeon creation and streamlines a lot of the campaign experience. Ideally, once everyone is ready to play, there shouldn’t be too many distractions here and the gameplay experience should be simple and smooth.*

After player/enemy/towns/dungeons are generated, the game will begin in Cader Idris and players will have opportunities to interact with each other and with the other inhabitants of Cader Idris. The first day involves “preparations” where players decide if they’re going to band together or journey apart for the adventure.
