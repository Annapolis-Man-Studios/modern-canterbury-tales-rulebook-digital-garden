Hello! My name is Andrew Gorbaty. I run my sole-proprietorship, Annapolis Man Studios, and I am the creator of this game. If you’ve known me for at least some time, gaming has had a long mainstay in my life. I’ve played a lot of video games, including fighting games, rhythm games, RPGs, strategy games, so on. While I love these games very much, I always wanted to engage in an in-person version of them. Luckily in college, a few of my friends introduced me to tabletop gaming. I reminisced over old times with a few friends recently and that inspired me to get involved in tabletop gaming again. The problem is that the last time I played a tabletop game, I played [Pathfinder](https://paizo.com/pathfinder), a homebrew version of D&D. While I had a lot of fond memories playing Pathfinder, I never enjoyed the intense number crunching and stat min-maxing involved with the game. Additionally, many days/weeks/months were required to finish one campaign. Given the number of other interests I have in my current life, I’m not sure committing to a Pathfinder/D&D campaign would be the best for me right now.


I’ve always had an interest in creating tabletop games, even when I was younger. In 2017, I attended [MAGFest](https://www.magfest.org/) and participated in an analog game jam. Over the course of an hour, I ended up creating a team-based randomized survival game involving paper cards drawn from a deck. To my chagrin, a few of you may have played this game. It left a lot to be desired, but I really loved the concept of having a co-op game like the one I had created. Hopefully this game hits the same sweet spot with a number of twists.
