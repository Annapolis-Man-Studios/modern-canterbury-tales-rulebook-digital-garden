Here lies the rules of Modern Canterbury Tales. Modern Canterbury Tales is a ai-powered tabletop that can be played online or in person. Please peruse these rules at your leisure.

If you have questions or concerns about the rules, please send an email to agorbz@annapolismanstudios.com so we can address your inquiry as quickly as possible!

We hope you have a lot of fun playing this interesting take on DnD style of tabletop RPGs!

*NOTE: There are mentions in the rulebook about the "Campaign Creator tool" throughout. This tool will be released sometime before the end of 2026. Please stay tuned for more updates.*
