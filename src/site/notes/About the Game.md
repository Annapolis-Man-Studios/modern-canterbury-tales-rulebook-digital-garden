title: About the Game
---

This game is a turn-based tabletop RPG with some characteristics of point and click adventures. The estimated duration of every game should be around 2-3 hours. The game can be either played on Discord or in-person. The game plays like D&D and other tabletop based games but with some key distinctions:

- The battle system is not grid based and is turn based based on semi-randomized encounters. This is to simplify the game so no time is spent dice rolling or including numerous other character stats into fight calculations.
    
- The game has 10-20 turns where each turn is a day in in-game time. A turn can be anywhere from 10-120 minutes depending on the players and their choices.
    
- Every participant has a character who has a special trait associated with them. The villains are designated by traits that resemble the [seven deadly traits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_deadly_sins) found in Catholic literature. Any other traits but these are designated as the heroes. There are also players that can be neutral, meaning they are neither heroes nor villains. The players do not know each other’s traits. Only the DM knows everyone’s traits at the start of the game. Players are allowed to reveal what their trait is at the start of the game if they wish, however that is not recommended for strategy reasons.
