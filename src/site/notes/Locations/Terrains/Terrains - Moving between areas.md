title: Terrains - Moving between areas
---

A terrain might look like this:

| Area | Type      | Content                                     |
| :--- | :-------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Start     | Entryway: The Foothills of the Mist.        |
| 2    | Encounter | Ambush: 2x Fog-Stalking Hares.              |
| 3    | Rest      | Shelter: A sheltered cave alcove (+2 HP).   |
| 4    | Treasure  | Ruined Shrine: Found an Old Silver Sigil.   |
| 5    | Encounter | Combat: 1x Stone-Crag Sentinel.             |
| 6    | Rest      | Cairn: A place of quiet reflection (+3 HP). |
| 7    | Encounter | Ambush: 2x Shadow-Mist Wisps.               |
| 8    | Boss      | Summit: The Guardian of the Veil.           |
In this case, the party begins the terrain at area 1 and makes their way through all section up to and including area 8. Each area contains one of the following:
- Encounter (battle/boss)
- Rest Area
- Treasure

Ending a turn in a location that is not a rest area is prohibited unless you've already defeated the enemy/boss in that area.

*NOTE TO DMs: Do not show the terrain map to the party unless a player uses an item that allows players to see the terrain map to peek at the encounters ahead.*
