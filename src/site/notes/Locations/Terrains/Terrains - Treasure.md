title: Terrains - Treasure
---

Sometimes, there are areas of a terrain which contain treasure. Similar to dungeons, treasure can either contain attachable items, items with a limited number of uses, or gold.

The format of areas with treasure look like so:
	
	| 4 | Treasure | Ruined Shrine: Found an Old Silver Sigil. 

This means there is treasure located in area 4 of the current terrain.
