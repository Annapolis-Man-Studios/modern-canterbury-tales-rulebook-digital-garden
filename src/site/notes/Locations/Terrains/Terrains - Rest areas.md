title: Terrains - Rest areas
---

Rest areas are a way to heal a small amount of health and take a break from battling. The party can also choose to end the current turn and heal a small amount of health (5 HP) and continue the terrain from where they stopped.

An example rest area format can look something like this:

	| 6 | Rest | Cairn: A place of quiet reflection (+3 HP). |

This means in area 6 a rest area resides.
