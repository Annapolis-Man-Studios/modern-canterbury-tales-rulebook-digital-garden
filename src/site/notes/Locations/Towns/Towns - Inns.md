title: Towns - Inns
---

Inns are places where players can restore their health. Sometimes townspeople will reside in the inns and will be able to be spoken to. To stay at an inn, there is usually a price in gold to pay unless there is some other event that renders this otherwise. This is up to the discretion of the DM.
