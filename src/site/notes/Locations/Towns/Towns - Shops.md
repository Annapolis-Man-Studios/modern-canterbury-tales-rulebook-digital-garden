title: Towns - Shops
---

Shops are places where players can by either attachable or n-time use items. When a shop for a town is generated, it has output similar to this:

	| Item Name | Type | Effect | Cost |
	| Border-Steel Blade | Weapon | +15 Attack | 1,200 Gold |
	| Reinforced Chainmail | Armor | +15 Defense | 1,200 Gold |
	| Marcher’s Boots | Footwear | +10 Speed | 800 Gold |
	| Treatise on Border Law| Book | +5 Diplomacy | 1,500 Gold |
	| Refined Whetstone | Item | +5 Attack | 400 Gold |

All items will cost a certain amount of gold unless otherwise specified. Players are allowed to barter for items at discretion of the DM.
