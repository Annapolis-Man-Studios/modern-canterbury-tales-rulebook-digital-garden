title: Towns - Townspeople
---

In every town, townspeople are present which the players can interact with. The DM can add as many or as few townspeople as they desire. However, in each town there are townspeople which are required to be present. When the town is generated, these townspeople will be in this format:

	### 3. Townspeople & Sidequests

	#### A. Sir Cedric (The Weary Knight)
	*   Location: Leaning against the exterior wall of the Armory.
	*   Dialog: "The border is porous, traveler. Every night, something crosses from the dark woods, and every morning, my men find the gates slightly ajar. I’m too old to chase shadows."
	*   Sidequest: "The Traitor’s Key"
	    *   Objective: Investigate the gatehouse at midnight.
	    *   Reward: A *Masterwork Shield* (+20 Defense) and 500 Gold.
	
	#### B. Elara the Scribe
	*   Location: Sitting near the town fountain, surrounded by scrolls.
	*   Dialog: "Trade is down because the mountain pass is blocked by rockslides. If those roads cleared, I could finalize the peace treaty documents, but the local stonemasons are too terrified of the 'Mountain Spirits' to work."
	*   Sidequest: "Clearing the Path"
	    *   Objective: Defeat the Earth Elementals blocking the Northern Pass.
	    *   Reward: *Diplomat's Signet Ring* (+10 Diplomacy) and a discount on all items in the "Iron & Ink" shop.
	
	#### C. Old Man Pip
	*   Location: Pacing back and forth near the town gates.
	*   Dialog: "Don't trust the merchants! They sell you boots that fall apart in the mud and blades that turn to lead when the cold sets in! Keep your coin for the blacksmith, he’s the only honest soul here."
	*   Dialog (If spoken to repeatedly): If you give him 10 Gold, he reveals a hidden vendor in the back alley who sells rare "Stolen Goods" (temporary buffs for boss encounters).

The DM must use the dialog provided (or something similar) for each of these townspeople. These townspeople have sidequests associated with them and the player must be given the option to pursue the designated sidequest upon speaking to them.
