title: Towns - Basic Rules
---

A town is an area where shops, inns, townspeople, and potential sidequests reside. All of these except for sidequest content will be generated by the Campaign Creator. Players are allowed to interact with these entities with supervision from the DM.

Descriptions for each part of the town are provided. Players are provided with all available inns, shops, and townspeople upon entering a new town.
