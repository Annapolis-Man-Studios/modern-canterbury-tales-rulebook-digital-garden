title: Towns - Sidequests
---

Sidequests are opportunities for players to engage in battles, puzzles, and other activities while within a town. Successfully completing these sidequests can provide participating players with powerful items and stat buffs. The text for a sidequest might look like the following:

	Sidequest: "The Traitor’s Key"
	Objective: Investigate the gatehouse at midnight.
	Reward: A Masterwork Shield (+20 Defense) and 500 Gold.

This text should be used when generating the content for the sidequest. Sidequests have two different flavors, *with and without battles*. Whether the designated sidequest is a battle or not is up to the discretion of the DM, unless specified otherwise.

A sidequest that doesn't require a battle will look something like this:

	### Sidequest: "The Traitor’s Key"
	
	**Scenario Context:**
	You are 50% through your journey, having reached the fortified border of the Ironwood Province. A rumor persists among the common folk that the Captain of the Guard is smuggling stolen royal intelligence through the gatehouse precisely at midnight. You have secured a vantage point overlooking the courtyard.

	### The Sidequest: The Midnight Alignment (Timed Puzzle)
	
	Instead of a battle, you must disable the magical security wards protecting the gatehouse office before the Captain arrives. If you take too long, the guard rotation will catch you, and the quest will fail.
	
	**The Setup:**
	In front of you is a series of four rotating dials etched with celestial symbols. Beneath them is an inscription: *"Only when the stars align with the shadows of the past shall the gates yield their secrets."*
	
	**The Puzzle Logic:**
	You have **60 seconds** of real-time to complete the following logic sequence. If you fail, the "Traitor's Key" is locked away for good.
	
	1.  **Dial 1 (Sun):** Rotate to the "Eclipse" position (pointing North).
	2.  **Dial 2 (Moon):** Rotate to the "New Moon" position (pointing South).
	3.  **Dial 3 (Star):** Rotate to the "North Star" position (pointing East).
	4.  **Dial 4 (Void):** Rotate to the "Abyss" position (pointing West).
	
	**Instructions:**
	*   You must state the order in which you turn the dials out loud (or write them down) as you "interact" with them.
	*   Once you begin, you must finish the sequence in under 60 seconds.
	*   If you stop to hesitate or look away for more than 5 seconds, the mechanism resets.

	### Success Outcome:
	Upon completing the sequence, the heavy iron door clicks open. Inside, you find no gold, but rather the **Masterwork Shield (+20 Defense)** hanging on the wall as a display piece, and a hidden ledger containing the evidence of treason, which you can trade to the local Magistrate for **500 Gold**.
	
	### Failure Outcome (or Time Expired):
	The gatehouse alarm triggers. You are forced to flee the area immediately to avoid being captured. The security mechanism locks permanently, and you lose the opportunity to claim the Shield or the reward.

The same sidequest generated as a battle looks similar to something like this:

	### Sidequest: "The Traitor’s Key"
	
	**Scenario:** You have received a cryptic note claiming that the Captain of the Guard is smuggling state secrets out of the capital. According to the informant, the exchange happens at the stroke of midnight inside the abandoned East Gatehouse.
	
	**Objective:** Investigate the gatehouse at midnight.
	
	---
	
	### Phase 1: The Infiltration (Exploratory - No Dice Rolls)
	As you reach the East Gatehouse at exactly midnight, you find the heavy oak door slightly ajar. You do not need to roll for stealth; your reputation as a seasoned traveler allows you to slip inside without alerting the guards stationed outside. 
	
	Inside, you find a sequence of three pressure-sensitive stone tiles on the floor. 
	*   **The Logic:** Stepping on the tiles in the wrong order triggers a loud alarm. 
	*   **The Solution:** Based on the cryptic note, you must step on the tiles in order of the **"Moon’s Phases"** etched into the walls: **Crescent (Left), Half (Center), Full (Right).** By following this sequence, the inner chamber unlocks silently.
	
	---
	
	### Phase 2: The Confrontation (Battle)
	Upon entering the inner chamber, you find the Captain of the Guard handing a cache of stolen documents to a masked mercenary. They turn to see you. The Captain flees, leaving you to deal with his hired muscle.
	
	**Enemy Stats: The Masked Mercenary**
	*   **Health:** 150
	*   **Attack:** 30
	*   **Defense:** 10
	*   **Speed:** 15
	*   **Diplomacy:** 80 (If your current player "Charisma" stat is 15 or higher, you may attempt to bribe the mercenary with 100 Gold to walk away, ending the battle immediately without combat.)
	
	---
	
	### Phase 3: The Discovery (Anti-Climactic Occurrence)
	After the mercenary is defeated (or bribed), you search the room for the documents. You find the folder, but as you pick it up, you accidentally knock over a crate of ancient, dust-covered medical supplies.
	
	*   **The Occurrence:** A hidden vial of "Alchemical Adrenaline" shatters, and you inhale the potent fumes. 
	*   **Effect:** For your next major boss battle, your **Speed stat is increased by +10**, but you feel "jittery," causing your **Attack stat to be reduced by -5** due to shaky hands.
	
	---
	
	### Reward
	You find a loose floorboard beneath where the mercenary stood, revealing the **Masterwork Shield (+20 Defense)** and a pouch containing **500 Gold**. You also secure the documents, which will be vital for the main storyline once you return them to the High Council.

For all sidequests the DM is inclined to follow the instructions provided with some flexibility.

*NOTE: By pursuing a sidequest, the in-game turn will end after the sidequest is resolved unless an item is used to prolong the turn.*
