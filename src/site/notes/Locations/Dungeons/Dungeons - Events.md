title: Dungeons - Events
---

Events occur in certain rooms which are usually status changes that impact the whole party. For example, given this map:

|           | Col 1             | Col 2               | Col 3               | Col 4             |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------------ | :------------------ | :---------------- |
| **Row 1** | [Start]           | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Treasure]          | [Event: Slippery] |
| **Row 2** | [Enemy: Cave Bat] | [Trap Door]         | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Empty]           |
| **Row 3** | [Event: Healing]  | [Enemy: Cave Bat]   | [Empty]             | [Treasure]        |
| **Row 4** | [Empty]           | [Treasure]          | [Event: Exhaustion] | [Boss]            |
The status effects could be the following:

	*   **Status (Slippery):** "The floor is coated in slick, black moss." (Defense Down)
	*   **Status (Healing):** "A gentle, warm glow emanates from a crystalline spring." (Heal All HP)
	*   **Status (Exhaustion):** "The air is thin and stale." (Attack Down)

If the specific stat number is not mentioned for an event that affects party character stats, DM should propose a number and inflict that on the party. Otherwise please follow all directions that are provided by the content generated by the Campaign Creator tool.
