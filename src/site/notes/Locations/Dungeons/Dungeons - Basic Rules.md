title: Dungeons - Basic Rules
---

In Modern Canterbury Tales, players will be required to complete dungeons as a team on their way to Camelot. Each dungeon is presented as a MxN grid where the players traverse as a team unless otherwise indicated. A sample dungeon layout could look like something like this:

	### The Giant’s Cavern of Rhyd Ddu
	**Dungeon Overview:** Located deep in the mountains, this cavern is home to stone-skinned giants. Being 25% into the game, the difficulty is moderate, testing party coordination and resource management.
	
	*   **Structure:** 2 Floors
	*   **Grid:** 4x4 (16 rooms per floor)
	*   **Traversals:** North, South, East, West
	
	### Floor 1: The Miner’s Descent
	*A damp, echoing cavern floor filled with discarded tools and stalactites.*

|           | Col 1             | Col 2               | Col 3               | Col 4             |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------------ | :------------------ | :---------------- |
| **Row 1** | [Start]           | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Treasure]          | [Event: Slippery] |
| **Row 2** | [Enemy: Cave Bat] | [Trap Door]         | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Empty]           |
| **Row 3** | [Event: Healing]  | [Enemy: Cave Bat]   | [Empty]             | [Treasure]        |
| **Row 4** | [Empty]           | [Treasure]          | [Event: Exhaustion] | [Stairs to F2]    |

---

	### Floor 2: The Giant’s Sanctum
	*Large, carved archways and massive stone furniture indicate the giants' living quarters.*

|           | Col 1             | Col 2                | Col 3                | Col 4                   |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------------- | :------------------- | :---------------------- |
| **Row 1** | [Empty]           | [Enemy: Elite Golem] | [Treasure]           | [Empty]                 |
| **Row 2** | [Event: Slippery] | [Empty]              | [Enemy: Elite Golem] | [Event: Sharp Stones]   |
| **Row 3** | [Treasure]        | [Enemy: Elite Golem] | [Empty]              | [Treasure]              |
| **Row 4** | [Empty]           | [Empty]              | [Empty]              | [BOSS: Giant Chieftain] |

The DM does not reveal the contents of each room to the players. However, players will be told where the starting location is located. The dungeon does not end until the boss is defeated.

Additionally, players are allowed to end a turn and replenish some health (5 HP) within the dungeon and start from where they left off, but all enemies will respawn.
