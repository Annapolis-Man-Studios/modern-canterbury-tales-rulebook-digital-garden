title: Dungeons - Room Inquiries
---

In many tabletop RPGs, players are able to ask for clue for each room of a dungeon to predict future occurrences. In Modern Canterbury Tales, each room of a dungeon has an associated clue generated for it. For example, given a dungeon map like this:

|           | Col 1             | Col 2               | Col 3               | Col 4             |
| :-------- | :---------------- | :------------------ | :------------------ | :---------------- |
| **Row 1** | [Start]           | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Treasure]          | [Event: Slippery] |
| **Row 2** | [Enemy: Cave Bat] | [Trap Door]         | [Enemy: Rock Golem] | [Empty]           |
| **Row 3** | [Event: Healing]  | [Enemy: Cave Bat]   | [Empty]             | [Treasure]        |
| **Row 4** | [Empty]           | [Treasure]          | [Event: Exhaustion] | [Boss]            |
the clues could be the following:

	  **Room Clues:**
	    *   **Start:** "The air is cold and smells of wet slate."
	    *   **Enemy (Golem):** "The sound of grinding boulders echoes from ahead."
	    *   **Enemy (Bat):** "High-pitched screeches bounce off the walls."
	    *   **Treasure:** "A metallic glint flickers in the dim light."
	    *   **Status (Slippery):** "The floor is coated in slick, black moss." (Defense Down)
	    *   **Status (Healing):** "A gentle, warm glow emanates from a crystalline spring." (Heal All HP)
	    *   **Status (Exhaustion):** "The air is thin and stale." (Attack Down)
	    *   **Trap Door:** "The ground beneath you feels hollow and loose."

**NOTE to DM: It is recommended to only provide the clues to the party generated by the Campaign Creator. There are opportunities for flexibility if the DM's wishes to be more creative with some of the clues.**
