title: Dungeons - Battles
---

All dungeons contain many enemies and one boss enemy. There can be 1-N enemies in each room containing enemies in a dungeon. There can never be more than one enemy in the boss room unless otherwise specified (e.g. boss has attack that summons weaker enemies).

For each dungeon, the DM will see a guide to follow for each enemy that appears. The enemy stats might look something like this:

| Enemy | Health | Attack | Defense | Speed | Diplomacy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cave Bat** | 15 | 8 | 2 | 10 | 15 |
| **Rock Golem** | 40 | 15 | 10 | 3 | 5 |
| **Elite Golem** | 65 | 22 | 15 | 4 | 2 |
The boss might will look like this:
	
	Boss: Rhyd Ddu Chieftain
	*   **Health:** 250
	*   **Attack:** 35
	*   **Defense:** 20
	*   **Speed:** 6
	*   **Diplomacy:** 0 (Boss cannot be negotiated with)
	*   **Special Attack: "Earthquake Slam"** - Hits all party members for 20 damage and reduces party speed by 5 for the next 2 turns.

All enemies will have "drops" which are either items, gold, or both. The drops might look something like this:

	Room Effects & Loot
	*   **Enemies:**
	    *   Bats: Drop 5 Gold.
	    *   Golems: Drop 20 Gold / Stone Fragments.
	    *   Chieftain: Drops 500 Gold / "Chieftain’s Club" (Weapon).

Many times the items won't have stat numbers. In these cases please leave the actual amount at the discretion of the DM. Otherwise please follow the amounts that are suggested by the Campaign Creator.
