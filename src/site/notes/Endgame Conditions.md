title: Endgame Conditions
---

- If the group arrives at Camelot and there is at least one villain in the group that arrives at Camelot, the villains must face the heroes in battle with villains having first strike priority
    
- If the group arrives at Camelot and there are no villains present, the heroes win
    
- If all heroes are eliminated before arrival in Camelot, the villains win
    
- If all villains are eliminated before arrival in Camelot, the heroes win
    
- If 20 turns elapses without anyone reaching Camelot, no one wins and the game ends in a draw

- If all party members fall in battle at the same time, the entire party losses the game 

*NOTE: "Arrival at Camelot" means the players complete the town/dungeon/terrain closest to the city of Camelot. This location should be noted when towns/dungeons/terrains are generated.*
