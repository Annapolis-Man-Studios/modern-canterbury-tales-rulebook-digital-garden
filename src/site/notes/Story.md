Sometime in the near future, scientists discover how time travel can be implemented and are able to build time machines. Some time traveling terrorists from the future wish to alter the course of history with regards to Great Britain because they see them as the greatest contributor to human suffering throughout all of human history. They plan to do this by creating a time paradox by time traveling to the 6th century and eliminating King Arthur. On the other hand, MI6, the CIA, and other agencies get wind of this intel and summon the greatest warriors to stop these terrorists. Their mission is to warn King Arthur of these terrorists and thwart them.

The one problem is all participants in this endeavor are not only teleported to the same town, but none of the participants know for sure who is a villain or who is a hero. Additionally, 6th century England is filled with ferocious monsters, bloodthirsty bandits, and other distasteful creatures. They must band together to trek from their starting location in the middle of nowhere Wales to King Arthur’s current location, Camelot. Will the heroes discover who the terrorists are in their party before they arrive in Camelot? Or will the terrorists hailing from the future assassinate King Arthur and alter the course of human history?
