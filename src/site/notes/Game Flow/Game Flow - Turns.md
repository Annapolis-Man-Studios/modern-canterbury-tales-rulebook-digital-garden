title: Game Flow - Turns
---

A turn represents the passage of one day in in-game time. After a certain number of turns have passed the game ends. When the party goes to sleep for the day (in either a town or a terrain) The number of turns before ending a game is usually up to the discretion of the DM, but it's recommended the game last for no more than 20 turns.

Passage of a turn:
- At the beginning of a turn, the party chooses any location adjacent to their current location. 
	- On the first turn, the party does not choose the starting location and cannot travel until turn 2
	- The party can opt to stay in the same location for more than one day
- The party will play through the new location until the party decides to go to rest for the day.
- When the party goes to rest for the day, the turn completes. All party members replenish 5 HP at the start of a new turn.
