title: Game Flow - Alliances
---

Each character in the party has an assigned alliance. Party members should not be inclined to reveal their assigned alliances when the game begins. This can be either determined by the Campaign Creator tool on character generation or the DM. Alliances can be one of three designations:
- Hero - As a hero, your goal is to arrive at Camelot with at least one hero standing. Your goal is to protect King Arthur at all costs.
- Villain - As a villain, your goal is to eliminate all heroes in your party before reaching Camelot. If the party ends the current turn in Camelot without all heroes eliminated, a battle between all standing Heroes and Villains occurs. In this battle, villains will always get first strike.
- Neutral - As a neutral participant, you don't have a specific goal. If a battle between Heroes and Villains ensues after ending the turn in Camelot, neutral participants must choose to side with either the Heroes or the Villains.

Upon their health being depleted to 0 and being eliminated from the campaign, a character's alliance will be revealed to the party.
