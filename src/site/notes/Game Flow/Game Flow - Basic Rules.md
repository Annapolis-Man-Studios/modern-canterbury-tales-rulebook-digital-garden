title: Game Flow - Basic Rules
---

In Modern Canterbury Tales, a party of heroes, villains, and neutral bystanders must band together and travel to Camelot with the hopes of aiding or supplanting King Arthur's rule. On the way to Camelot, players will travel between towns, dungeons, and terrains. Towns are bustling with villagers needing help with tasks and dungeons and terrains are crawling with powerful monsters to defeat.
